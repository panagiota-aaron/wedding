Frequently asked questions

Συχνές ερωτήσεις

How do I RSVP?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

In your formal invitation, you will find a number of RSVP cards respective to the number of guests in your party. You may RSVP by sending your RSVP card to us directly. An envelope with our address has been included in the invitation for your convenience.

How long do I have to RSVP?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

We kindly ask that you RSVP before August 15th. If we do not receive any correspondence from you by this date, we will assume that you have respectfully declined the invitation to attend the wedding.

Can I bring a plus one?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

We kindly ask that only guests named on their formal invitation be in attendance at the wedding. We appreciate your understanding.

Can children attend?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

We love and adore all the little ones in our lives. To honour our intent for an intimate evening, we have chosen to host an adults-only wedding.

When should I arrive at the church?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

We kindly ask that all guests arrive at the church by 2:45PM at the latest.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

We kindly ask that phones be silenced and put away as the ceremony takes place.

How should I get from the ceremony venue to the reception venue?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

The reception hall is located approximately 15 minutes from the church, and is accessible by car, uber, taxi, and public transportation. For guests staying both Friday and Saturday night, an alternative shuttle service is available on a first come, first serve basis. More information can be found in the Transportation section on the details page.

Where should I park at the reception venue?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

There is unfortunately no parking available on site for guests attending the wedding reception only. For additional details on reception parking, please visit the Parking section on the details page.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

Please let us know of any dietary restrictions or allergies when submitting your RSVP. We want to ensure everyone can comfortably enjoy the evening!

Who should I contact for any other questions I may have?

Πώς μπορώ να επιβεβαιώσω την παρουσία μου;

Should you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out by calling or texting Panagiota-Patricia at (613) 290-6919 or Aaron at (613) 809-4103.
